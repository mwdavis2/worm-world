# CRISPR inversion balancers (Dejima 2018)

Generated by `scripts/build-inversion-balancers.mjs` from Dejima et al. 2018,
Cell Reports 22:232-241 (`data/Inversion_strains.pdf`): the 13 crossover
suppressors of Table 1 and the 25 strains of Table 2 that carry one of them and
a Pmyo-2 fluorescent marker. Output: `data/inversion_balancers/*.csv`. Import
in this order: `variations`, `phenotypes`, `alleles`, `allele_exprs`,
`expr_relations`, `strains`, `strain_alleles`.

## Balancers

Each balancer is one variation with a **single range**, from the positions of
its left and right genes (Table 1) in the gene table, placed at the range's
midpoint with its cM interpolated. The ranges match the paper's Mb values.
Table 1 misprints two genes (`snj-27` for `dnj-27`, `Y102A11A6` for
`Y102A11A.6`); the real names are used. An inversion has no partner half, so
there are no aneuploid rows.

## Alleles

- **Marker insertions** keep their tmIs names: the allele `tmIs1189` is the
  Pmyo-2::Venus insertion in `dpy-2`. Each expresses `Pmyo-2::Venus`,
  `Pmyo-2::mCherry` or `Pmyo-2::GFP` (dominant, one or two copies) and, where
  Table 2 shows the gene's own phenotype (dpy-2, dpy-5, unc-60, unc-5, unc-75,
  unc-49), a recessive class-5 phenotype (`Dpy-2`, `Unc-60`...) rescued by its
  wild-type row.
- **Deletions** `tm9715` (dpy-5), `tm9719` and `tm9718` (unc-9) and `tm750`
  (lig-4, no visible phenotype).
- **Breakpoint alleles** `tmCXmec`, `tmCXunc`, `tmCXlon`, one per gene whose
  breakpoint gives a visible phenotype in Table 2: tmC5 and tmC9 (mec-3,
  unc-31), tmC3 and tmC12 (lon-3, unc-23), tmC30 (lon-2, mec-10), tmC24
  (mec-10). Not made for the balancers without a visible phenotype or for
  tmC29's semi-Emb (no gene). The Unc of tmC3 is attributed to `unc-23`, the
  gene it shares with tmC12; `unc-83` is also at a tmC3 breakpoint.
- **Array** `tmEx4950` (strains FX30252 and FX30253): expresses `Pvha-6::gfp`
  and the wild-type `Unc-9` phenotype, which rescues the unc-9 deletion.

## Strains

Named by their FX number; each is its balancer, the balancer's breakpoint
alleles and the strain's own alleles, homozygous (nothing is lethal). The
stored genotype text is checked against `Strain.toString` by
`src/models/frontend/Strain/inversionBalancers.test.ts`.

Not included: the plain balancers, the deletion-only variants (no Pmyo-2),
`tmC1` (not one of the 13), the older `tmIn` inversions and the marker-only
strains.
